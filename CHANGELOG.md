# Changelog

## Gatherline 1.0.0

Gatherline is its own addon now (it was GatherPath Plus, a fork of GatherPath Forever).

- **New name and look**: Gatherline, `/gl` (or `/gatherline`), a dark flat window with a jade accent, its own logo, and a new menu: *Routes* (My routes, New route), *On the way* (Guidance, Map & lines, No-go areas) and *Gatherline* (About).
- **My routes** as cards: the route's colour as a strip on the left, Start up front, then small buttons to turn round, edit, rebuild and delete.
- **Guidance** gathers everything about Start, including the arrows and the line to the route (they were under Display).
- **About** shows whether GatherMate2, Shortest Path Forever and a walking map for your continent are there, brings routes over, and says what Gatherline is made from.
- **German and English**: follows the game's language.
- Taboo areas are called **no-go areas** now.
- **Routes come over** from GatherPath Plus or GatherPath Forever: by themselves the first time (Gatherline still empty, the other addon switched on), or with *Bring routes over* on About.
- Saved separately (`GatherlineDB`); GatherPath Plus and GatherPath Forever aren't needed.
